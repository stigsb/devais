# Ready-made 1.0 mm pitch SH-compatible double-ended cable assemblies

Research date: 2026-09-13. Every fact tagged VERIFIED (with source URL) or UNVERIFIED.

## F1. The key enabling fact: JST SR (IDC) housings mate with SH headers

There is **no JST catalog harness built from SH crimp housings (SHR-xxV-S-B) on both ends**.
JST's finished double-ended 1.0 mm catalog harness uses the **SR** series IDC housing
(`xxSR-3S`), which mates with the **same headers** as the SH crimp housing.

- VERIFIED — JST SH datasheet, feature list: "Socket compatible with IDC style SR connector".
  https://www.jst-mfg.com/product/pdf/eng/eSH.pdf
- VERIFIED — JST SR/SZ product page: "Compatible Crimp-style connector available (SH connector)".
  https://www.jst-mfg.com/product/detail_e.php?series=244
- VERIFIED — DigiKey JST product highlight: "The SR series IDC housings can mate with the SH series headers."
  https://www.digikey.com/en/product-highlight/j/jst/sh-and-sr-series-connectors

So if the board footprints are standard SH headers (`SM0xB-SRSS-TB` side-entry or
`BM0xB-SRSS-TB` top-entry), the JST `AxxSRxxSR30K…` harness plugs straight in.

### Consequences vs. an SH crimp harness
| | SH crimp (SHR-xxV-S-B + SSH-003T-P0.2-H) | SR IDC (xxSR-3S, catalog harness) |
|---|---|---|
| Current rating | 1.0 A AC/DC (AWG #28) — VERIFIED, eSH.pdf | 0.7 A AC/DC — VERIFIED, eSR_SZ.pdf |
| Wire | AWG #32–#28, insulation OD 0.4–0.8 mm — VERIFIED, eSH.pdf | AWG #30 only, 7 strands tin-coated annealed copper, insulation OD 0.54–0.58 mm — VERIFIED, eSR_SZ.pdf |
| Voltage | 50 V | 50 V — VERIFIED |
| Temp | −25 to +85 °C | −25 to +85 °C — VERIFIED |

0.7 A per contact is the only downgrade. For the 8-pin USB harness with VBUS and GND
doubled up (2 contacts each), that is 1.4 A of headroom — adequate.

## F2. Part number scheme (confirmed)

`A` + NN + `SR` + NN + `SR` + `30` + `K` + LLL + `A`/`B`

- `A` — assembly prefix
- first `NN SR` — positions + housing series, end 1
- second `NN SR` — positions + housing series, end 2
- `30` — wire gauge, AWG 30 (UL1571, black) — VERIFIED on JST drawings GAM-286/GAM-287
- `K` — separator
- `LLL` — wire length in mm: `51` (2"), `102` (4"), `152` (6"), `203` (8"), `254` (10"), `305` (12") — VERIFIED, all six exist on DigiKey for 2/4/7/8 positions
- trailing letter — pin-out, see F3

Housings used are `02SR-3S`, `04SR-3S`, `07SR-3S`, `08SR-3S` (VERIFIED, GAM-286/287 Table 1).
`08SR-3S` alone is $0.53 at DigiKey, "CONN RCPT 8POS IDC 30AWG TIN" (VERIFIED,
https://www.digikey.com/en/products/result?keywords=08SR-3S).

## F3. ORIENTATION — DigiKey's label is the opposite of JST's pin-out. Read carefully.

From the JST manufacturing drawings (primary source, both fetched and rendered):

- **Suffix `A` = "Pin Out: 1 to 1"** — drawing GAM-286, title block reads
  "1.0mm Pitch SR Standard Catalog Assembly - 4" Overall Length - Pin Out: 1 to 1".
  The drawing shows PIN 1 marked on the **same (top) edge at both ends**.
  VERIFIED — https://gam-gec.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/GAM-286.pdf
- **Suffix `B` = "Pin Out: 1 to N"** — drawing GAM-287, same title but "Pin Out: 1 to N".
  Drawing shows PIN 1 on the top edge at the left end and on the **bottom edge** at the right end.
  VERIFIED — https://gam-gec.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/GAM-287.pdf

DigiKey's "Connector Type" attribute says the reverse of this:
- `A08SR08SR30K102A` → "Socket to Socket, **Reversed**" (VERIFIED, DigiKey 9922205)
- `A08SR08SR30K102B` → "Socket to Socket" (VERIFIED, DigiKey 9922206, DKPN 455-3656-ND)

Reconciliation (reasoned, not stated by either source — UNVERIFIED interpretation):
DigiKey is describing the **physical connector body orientation** (one housing flipped
relative to the other), JST is describing the **electrical net mapping**. With a flat
ribbon where all wires leave on one face, flipping one housing is exactly what produces
pin 1 → pin 1. The two statements are consistent once you read "Reversed" as "housing
flipped", not "pin-out reversed".

**Decision: order the `A` suffix for pin 1 → pin 1.** The JST drawing is the authoritative
statement and it is explicit. On DigiKey the `A` part will be listed as "Reversed" — expect
this and do not switch to `B` because of it.

Risk R1: if this interpretation is wrong, the `B` parts are the same price and in stock, so
buy one of each length for the first prototype build and buzz them out with a meter before
committing. Cost of hedging: about $2.

Both suffixes are physically identical otherwise: 30 AWG UL1571 black wire, 101.6 ±5.0 mm
wire length / 108.8 ±5.0 mm overall for the `102` size (VERIFIED, GAM-286/287).
Wire colour: **all black, no colour coding** — DigiKey colour attribute is
"Black, Individual" (VERIFIED). Pin 1 is identified by the housing marking only.

## F7. Other sources considered

- **DigiKey custom cable service** — DigiKey Guntersville does automated crimp and IDC
  terminations on JST connectors. Mentioned on
  https://www.digikey.com/en/product-highlight/j/jst/idc-assemblies-and-pre-crimped-leads
  (VERIFIED that the service exists). Pricing/MOQ/turnaround UNVERIFIED. Not needed — the
  catalog parts cover every position count required.
- **Amazon / AliExpress generic "JST SH 1.0mm 8-pin double-ended 10cm"** — not needed and
  not recommended. Generic listings frequently ship the reversed pin-out and rarely state
  which. If one is used anyway: photograph both ends with the latch/ramp face up; if the
  red (or pin-1-marked) wire sits on the same edge at both ends with both housings latch-up,
  the cable is reversed; if pin 1 is on opposite edges with both latch-up, it is 1-to-1.
  Always buzz it out. This paragraph is reasoning, UNVERIFIED against any listing.
